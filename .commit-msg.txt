PushKit registration. Under TrollStore, aps-environment has no valid
APNs signature so registration always fails and the system terminates
the app at launch (instant crash on icon tap).

- entitlements: remove aps-environment / aps-connection-initiate
- project.yml: remove -framework PushKit

crash when PushKit is never registered)
